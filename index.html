About me

Hi, I’m Jiya Rathod.

I’m a B.Tech AIML student at Universal AI University, Karjat. I turn Figma designs into interfaces that feel as good as they look. I’ve worked on front-end projects and design systems, and I pay close attention to details like palettes, empty states, and how a screen behaves when someone actually uses it. I’m aiming for front-end and product design roles, where I can keep working on the part of the product people see and touch.
